Frequently Asked Questions

What is Fan League Tap?

Fan League Tap is a casual football fan competition game. You choose a league, pick a team, and tap to add fan support to your team's in-app score.

Are the rankings official league standings?

No. Rankings are based only on in-app fan support. They are not official football league standings and do not represent real match results.

Why was my rewarded ad boost not applied immediately?

Rewarded ad boosts are applied after ad verification is completed. If your connection is unstable, please wait a moment and reopen the app.

Can I change my nickname?

You can update your nickname from the supporter rankings area in the app. Nicknames are used only for fan ranking display.

The app is not loading. What should I do?

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Make sure you are using the latest app version.
  • Restart the app or your device.
  • If the issue continues, email us with your device model and app version.

How can I delete my data?

Email vahapunlu@gmail.com with your deletion request. We aim to process requests within 30 days.
