Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fan League Tap and other Derby Tap Games applications collect, use, and protect information.

Fan League Tap rankings are based only on in-app fan support. They are not official football league standings and do not represent real match results.

Information We Collect

Device and App Information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, and language settings.
  • Device or app identifiers used for app functionality, fraud prevention, and advertising where permitted.
  • App integrity and verification signals, including Firebase App Check related information.
  • Crash diagnostics and basic app usage events used to improve stability and understand app performance.

Game Data

  • Selected league and team.
  • Tap counts, fan scores, reward usage, ranking data, and season results.
  • Nickname if you choose to set one.

Advertising Data

  • Ad interaction and reward verification data through Google AdMob.
  • Rewarded ad validation data used to prevent invalid reward claims.

How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve the game experience.
  • To store scores, calculate rankings, and display fan leaderboards.
  • To apply rewarded ad boosts after ad verification.
  • To protect the app from fraud, automated abuse, and invalid requests.
  • To respond to support and data deletion requests.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display ads and verify rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Firebase

We use Firebase services such as App Check, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, and Crashlytics to protect the app, support notifications, understand app usage, and improve stability. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

Cloudflare

We use Cloudflare to host backend services and store game data required for rankings, score processing, and reward validation.

Data Retention

Game data is kept as long as needed to operate fan rankings, seasonal results, abuse prevention, and support requests.

Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data by emailing vahapunlu@gmail.com. We aim to process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are intended for a general audience and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.
  • On iOS, App Tracking Transparency permission is requested before tracking where required.
  • You can contact us to request data deletion.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at vahapunlu@gmail.com.

This policy applies to Fan League Tap and Derby Tap Games applications.
