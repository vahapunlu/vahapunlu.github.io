About Fan League Tap

Fan League Tap is a casual football fan competition game. Players choose a country league, pick a team, tap to support their side, and follow team and supporter rankings.

Fan League Tap rankings are based only on in-app fan support. They are not official football league standings and do not represent real match results.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fan League Tap and other Derby Tap Games applications collect, use, and protect information.

Information we collect

  • Device and app information, such as device type, operating system, app version, and app integrity signals.
  • Game data, such as selected league, selected team, tap counts, fan scores, reward usage, nickname, and ranking data.
  • Crash diagnostics and basic app usage events used to improve stability and understand app performance.
  • Advertising data related to Google AdMob ad delivery, rewarded ads, and ad verification.
  • Support information you send to us by email.

How we use information

  • To run the game, store scores, maintain rankings, and apply reward boosts.
  • To protect the service from fraud, automated abuse, and invalid reward claims.
  • To show ads and verify rewarded ad completion through Google AdMob.
  • To respond to support requests and data deletion requests.

Third-party services

We use Google AdMob for advertising and rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and use data as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

We use Firebase services such as App Check, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, and Crashlytics to protect the app, support notifications, understand app usage, and improve stability. See Firebase Privacy and Security.

We use Cloudflare to host backend services and store game data required for rankings and score processing.

Data retention and deletion

Game data is kept as long as needed to operate fan rankings and seasonal results. You may request deletion of your data by emailing vahapunlu@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

Our apps are intended for a general audience and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • On iOS, App Tracking Transparency permission is requested before tracking where required.
  • You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.
